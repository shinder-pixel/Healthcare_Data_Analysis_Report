# Healthcare_Data_Analysis_Report
## Project Overview: 

This project analyzes the financial performance of a healthcare center and evaluates healthcare provider performance using a comprehensive healthcare dataset.

The objective is to transform raw healthcare data into meaningful financial, operational, and provider-level insights through data analysis and an interactive dashboard.

The analysis focuses on revenue, expenses, profitability, patient activity, provider efficiency, and trends over time to support data-driven decision-making.

## KPI and Trends-

1. Identify and present the key performance indicators (KPIs) and significant trends in the dataset.
2. Detailed Analysis: Ensure the analysis is thorough and detailed, covering all relevant aspects of the healthcare center's finance and the performance of healthcare providers.
3. Dashboard Creation: Create a comprehensive dashboard consisting of:
- Financial Overview: Summarize the financial health of the healthcare center.
- Provider Insights: Analyze the performance and efficiency of healthcare providers.
- Trend Analysis: Highlight any important trends over time.
- Additional Insights: Any other relevant insights that can be derived from the data.
- 
Expectations:
- Visualization: Use appropriate visualizations to effectively communicate the findings.
- Interactivity: Ensure the dashboard is interactive and user-friendly.
- Clarity: Present the data in a clear and concise manner, making it easy for stakeholders to understand the insights.

  ## Project Insight:
  
-The healthcare center generated approximately £4M in total billing.

-Treatment cost accounts for approximately £3M, making it the largest major cost component shown.

-Insurance coverage totals approximately £2.23M, indicating that insurance contributes a substantial portion toward healthcare expenses.

-Out-of-pocket payments are approximately £2.01M, showing that patients still bear a significant share of healthcare costs.

-Medication costs are approximately £546K, while room charges account for around £1.0M.

-The average billing amount is approximately £851 per record, while the average treatment cost is £526.

-The average out-of-pocket amount is approximately £403.50, suggesting a meaningful direct financial burden on patients.

##Conclusion: 

In 2024 Average billing amount was approx.£690, while in 2025 it was approx. £1000, so there is increased total billing over a year.
From Northern Ireland there is maximum response.

In Diagnostic procedures X-Ray accounting for the 33% share of procedure billing.
Patients with HTN visited more followed by appendicitis and asthma.

From a departmental perspective, Orthopedics contributing 27% followed by Cardiology and General Surgery collectively account for the majority of the displayed departmental billing.

##Takeaway:
From patient perspective, need to focus on patients from England. Also there should be reduction in patients amount paying out of pocket.
Pending Billing: Approximately £2M in billing remains pending, representing a significant outstanding amount. Monitoring the aging and distribution of these pending bills can help identify collection gaps and improve cash-flow management.
